01 Road-map · from research to operations

Build the evidence.
Then expand autonomy.

Each stage earns the next. Model fidelity, uncertainty, operational constraints, and oversight are established before capability expands.

MODEL → VALIDATE → OPTIMIZE → INTEGRATE → AUTHORIZE
01

Scientific Modeling

BUILD THE COMPUTATIONAL FOUNDATION

Develop and validate Scientific Machine Learning, neural operators, scientific surrogate and reduced-order models, and differentiable simulation for complex physical systems.

Evaluate accuracy, computational efficiency, generalization, uncertainty, and validity within defined operating conditions.

OUTCOME

Validated models for scientific analysis, engineering simulation, and system prediction.

02

Decision Optimization

CONVERT PREDICTIONS INTO BETTER DECISIONS

Combine validated models with uncertainty quantification, scenario simulation, causal analysis where appropriate, and constrained mathematical optimization.

Compare alternatives against engineering requirements, economic objectives, resource constraints, and operational risks.

OUTCOME

Evidence-based decisions, explicit constraints, measurable objectives.

03

Computational Decision Systems

INTEGRATE INTELLIGENCE INTO OPERATIONS

Join computational models, operational data, simulation, optimization, and domain knowledge into deployable systems.

Establish integration requirements, performance indicators, validation procedures, monitoring, audit trails, and appropriate human oversight.

OUTCOME

Governed systems designed for industrial and enterprise workflows.

04

Bounded Autonomous Systems

ENABLE CONTROLLED OPERATIONAL EXECUTION

Integrate agentic AI, automated planning, workflow orchestration, and feedback to execute authorized tasks within defined operating boundaries.

Apply access controls, constraint enforcement, monitoring, escalation, and fallback mechanisms appropriate to application risk.

OUTCOME

Controlled automation with explicit permissions and accountable execution.

05

Advanced Intelligence Research

INVESTIGATE FUTURE CAPABILITIES

Explore continual learning, world models, generalizable reasoning, automated scientific discovery, and emerging computational architectures.

Evaluate with reproducible experiments, relevant benchmarks, comparative baselines, and documented limitations before production consideration.

OUTCOME

A research foundation for more adaptive, generalizable computational intelligence.
