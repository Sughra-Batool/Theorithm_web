Research Priorities

Advancing Computational Intelligence Through Scientific Evidence

Research in Progress
Emerging paradigm 01

01 Continual Learning and Adaptive Intelligence

Develop methods that enable computational systems to learn from new data, adapt to changing conditions, and retain previously acquired knowledge.

More adaptable computational systems that maintain performance as data, environments, and operating conditions evolve.

Emerging paradigm 02

02 World Models and Predictive Simulation

Develop computational models that represent system dynamics, simulate possible futures, and evaluate alternative actions under uncertainty.

Improved forecasting, scenario evaluation, and decision-making under changing conditions.

Emerging paradigm 03

03 Automated Scientific Discovery

Develop computational methods to generate hypotheses, design experiments, discover mathematical relationships, and identify governing principles from data and scientific evidence.

Accelerated scientific investigation, improved model discovery, and evidence-supported insights into complex systems.

Emerging paradigm 04

04 Assurance-native computing

A computational approach that incorporates cybersecurity, privacy, and AI safety alongside numerical reliability, uncertainty quantification, differentiable simulation, and constrained optimization. Candidate systems are assessed against explicit threat models, safety and privacy requirements, numerical error bounds, uncertainty calibration, and feasibility constraints.

A reproducible assurance case defining measurable acceptance criteria, evidence, and residual risks for each system—covering security, privacy, safety, numerical reliability, uncertainty, simulation validity, and constraint satisfaction.

Merging paradigm 05 Under evaluation

05 Quantum computing

A distinct computational model; potential application domains are assessed against algorithm fit, hardware requirements and maturity, and comparative performance.

A ranked set of candidate applications, supported by resource estimates, hardware-readiness criteria, and reproducible comparisons with relevant classical methods.

Emerging paradigm 06

06 Neuromorphic computing

A distinct brain-inspired paradigm; potential application domains are assessed against operating principles, hardware maturity, workload fit, energy-performance tradeoffs, and evidence.

A validated workload assessment reporting accuracy, latency, energy use, hardware readiness, and performance against an appropriate conventional-computing baseline.

Emerging paradigm 07 Under evaluation

07 Photonic computing

A distinct light-based paradigm; potential application domains are assessed against specific hardware requirements, technical maturity, integration constraints, and comparative performance.

A workload-to-hardware assessment that accounts for end-to-end performance, including optical-to-electronic conversion, data movement, integration requirements, and comparison with established platforms.
